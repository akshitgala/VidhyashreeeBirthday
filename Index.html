A LETTER — UNFILTERED

Read this when nobody’s looking.

Vidhyashree,

Today the world owes you cake, and somewhere, an ID checker still owes you an apology, because no, you don’t look 30+, you look 22, and we both know you’re going to milk that until at least the next concert.

I’ll keep reminding you I’m an entire one whole inch taller than you. Non-negotiable. It’s in the friendship contract, somewhere between “concerts only with each other” and “the others don’t need to know about that bar last Friday.”

You used to tell me everything. Lately the messages are shorter, the calls fewer. I’m not making a thing out of it, I just wanted you to know I noticed, and I’m still here. Same seat, same table, same playlist on shuffle.

So here’s the plan: Martin Garrix and Alan Walker were the warm-up. Ed Sheeran is the bridge. Coldplay is the chorus. We’re not missing any of it. Loud music, bad decisions, better memories, same as always, just us.

Happy birthday, partner-in-secret. The festival has only just started.

— Akshit
